### What the physical paper prototype is:
- The model is 8 Styrofoam cubes that are stuck on 8 separate wooden skewers, this holds the level and suspends it in the air.
- The player will hold a controller (or 2x2 Rubik's cube) and the 8 cubes will be set up the same as the level.
- We will have paper arrows to indicate which row and column is selected.

### How Players will Interface with the game:
- Optional: We present the PowerPoint prototype of the menu system.
- We give the player a controller or 2x2 Rubik's cube.
- We tell them they have to collect the key and walk into the goal door, which we point out to them.
- We explain the movement mechanics, and how to enter rotation mode.
- We get them to walk onto the rotate platform, then explain the mechanics to them, showing them the arrows which point at rows and columns.
- We point the arrows and ask them which way to rotate, allowing them to turn.
	- If they get lost, we will play the game for them.
	- Have a video ready which shows the controller inputs and the correct solution.
