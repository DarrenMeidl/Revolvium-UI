# Luca's UTSE
### Luca's Motivation
I see Revolvium as a relaxing and mentally stimulating puzzle game. It lets me engage in problem-solving without time constraints. I love the freedom to rotate and explore the level at my own pace, which aligns with my love for cubing. I love that the game doesn't require me to be timed, I choose to improve my time on my own accord. I am already very familiar with how cubes move, and understood how the complex controls mapped to my controller quite quickly.

### System
- The game allows me to switch between moving around and interact with the level, while the rotation mode is for changing the level.
- The visual feedback of rotating is clear, and key objects like doors and buttons are visible. There's no timing, which makes the experience more relaxing, which lets me have a mentally calm break from work.

###  Environment
Physical Setup:
- PlayStation 5
- PS5 Controller
- In front of a TV in his sitting room
- On the couch
Distractions:
- My work can send notifications to the group chat and I might want to pick up that shift, they change the drivers all the time.

# Lillith's UTSE
### Lillith's Motivation
I view Revolvium as a calming puzzle game that doesn't overwhelm me too much. As someone without cubing experience, I enjoy discovering new strategies at my own pace and watching other cubing videos in my spare time too. I love the game’s lack of time pressure. The puzzles feel challenging but not frustrating, which makes me want to play more, since it's so satisfying to figure it out, or even just stumble into the solution!

### System
- The game's controls are pretty hard for me to understand at first, it makes sense after I play around in each level for a while, but they are never something I can remember once the next level starts. Luckily the visual guides on screen are very useful for me!
- The game guides me enough to feel comfortable, and so far there hasn't been too many things in the level I have to remember at once.

###  Environment
Physical Setup:
- PC (Steam)
- Keyboard
- On monitor at bedroom desk
- Bedroom
Distractions:
- My mam always comes into my room, so I'm glad that I can pause the game at any time, or even just leave it unpaused since nothing happens without me touching it!
- My brother comes in to annoy me too, but he's easier to kick out.

# Mary's UTSE
### Mary's Motivation
I used to play all types of games, mostly fantasy stuff when I had both of my hands. Since I lost my left hand, I had to give up most of the action genres of games that I loved so much, and have since grown to playing slower games like puzzles to keep me entertained. I use a PS5 controller with a 3d printed attachment that lets me just use my right hand, so I can adapt to play a wider range of games, but my speed has still been greatly decreased.
I tried to do Rubik's cubes about 3 years ago, it was too much effort to turn the real ones, but speedcubes were much easier to turn, Revolvium seems like an opportunity to be able to play around with the idea in an easier way for me.

### System
- The controls are obvious for what level piece I am trying to turn around, but only after some playing around. The controls don't map as intuitively to my controller as they seem to on a regular one, but that's because my one is different. The arrows make it clear 
- I don't feel the need to know how a cube works for the level, but it's still fun and similar to when I did real cubes, it's much easier this time.
- The game is very easy to control, I don't need to hold multiple buttons or do something quickly.

### Environment
Physical Setup:
- PC (Steam)
- PS5 Controller with [one-handed attachment](https://www.ign.com/articles/dualsense-ps5-controller-one-hand-adapter-interview)
- On monitor at her desk
- In her house office
Distractions:
- I can get cramps in my hand after about an hour, so it's important that I can leave and come back at any point.
